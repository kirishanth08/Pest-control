Cockroach Control

Advanced gel bait and residual spray treatments to eliminate cockroaches at the source.

Overview

Cockroaches are one of the most common household pests, thriving in warm, moist environments like kitchens and bathrooms. They carry bacteria such as Salmonella and E. coli, contaminating food and surfaces. Our cockroach control service uses a combination of gel baiting and insect growth regulators (IGR) to target colonies at their source — not just the visible insects. We treat cracks, crevices, behind appliances, and all harborage points for lasting results.

Our Process

1

Inspection

Thorough assessment of your property to identify cockroach species, nesting sites, and entry points.

2

Treatment

Application of gel bait in critical areas, IGR to disrupt breeding, and residual spray along baseboards and joints.

3

Sealing

Caulking and sealing cracks, gaps around pipes, and potential entry points to block re-infestation.

Follow-Up

Free re-treatment within 30 days if pests return. Ongoing monitoring available.
