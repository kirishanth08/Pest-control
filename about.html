Our Story

How SafeGuard Was Founded

SafeGuard Pest Control was founded in 2010 by David Chen, a former entomologist who saw a need for science-based, eco-friendly pest control solutions in the residential and commercial market.

What started as a two-person operation serving local neighborhoods has grown into a team of 50+ certified technicians covering 25+ cities across the region. Our commitment to using EPA-approved, botanic-based products has made us the preferred choice for families and businesses alike.

Today, we continue to innovate with the latest Integrated Pest Management (IPM) techniques, ensuring effective treatments that are safe for your loved ones and the environment.
